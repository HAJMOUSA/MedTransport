# Driver App Guide

The MidTransport driver app runs on your phone. It shows your trips for the day, tracks your location while you drive, helps you navigate, and lets you confirm each pickup and drop-off with a quick code.

## Signing in

1. Open the MidTransport app.
2. Enter the email and password your dispatcher gave you → **Sign in**.
3. The app remembers you — you usually won't need to sign in again each day.

## Going on shift

- On the **trip list** screen, tap **Go on shift**.
- This tells dispatch you're available and starts location sharing so they can see you on the map.
- Tap **End shift** when you're done for the day. (Location sharing stops.)

> The app only shares your location while you're on shift.

## Your trips

The main screen lists **today's trips** in order. Each shows the rider, pickup and drop-off, the scheduled time (or **Will-call**), and any special needs (e.g. wheelchair). The list updates automatically when dispatch adds or changes a trip.

Tap a trip to open it.

## Running a trip (step by step)

1. **Start / En-route.** Open the trip and begin driving to the pickup. The app reports your GPS so dispatch sees your progress.
2. **Navigate.** Tap **Navigate** to open Google Maps / Apple Maps with the address preloaded.
3. **Arrival.** When you reach the pickup area, the app detects you've arrived. The rider automatically receives a **6-digit code** by text, and the app opens the **code entry** screen.
4. **Confirm pickup.** Ask the rider for their code, type it in, and submit. This records a verified, time-stamped, GPS-tagged pickup.
   - **No phone / no code?** Tap the **photo** option to take a timestamped photo instead. This still records proof of the pickup.
5. **Drive to drop-off.** Tap **Navigate** again for the destination. The trip status updates as you go.
6. **Confirm drop-off.** On arrival, the rider gets a drop-off code — enter it the same way (or use the photo fallback). The trip is now **Completed**.

Move on to your next trip. Everything you do updates the dispatcher's board and map in real time.

## Will-call trips

A **will-call** trip has no fixed pickup time — the rider calls when they're ready. It stays in your list; start it when dispatch tells you the rider is ready.

## Good habits

- **Go on shift** at the start and **End shift** at the end — this keeps the dispatcher's map accurate and saves battery.
- Keep **location permission** enabled for the app and keep the app open (or foregrounded) during active trips so GPS and arrival detection work.
- Always **confirm the code** (or take the fallback photo) — this is the official proof the trip happened and is needed for billing.
- Keep your phone charged; GPS + navigation use battery.

## If something goes wrong

- **Didn't get the code screen on arrival?** Make sure you're actually at the address and location is enabled; you can still reach code entry from the trip screen.
- **Rider has no phone?** Use the **photo** fallback on the code screen.
- **Wrong code / expired?** Codes expire after a few minutes — dispatch can resend, or use the photo fallback.
- **Can't sign in / app stuck?** Contact your dispatcher; see also [FAQ & Troubleshooting](faq-troubleshooting.md).
